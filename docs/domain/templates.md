# Part 5. Templates

## The one-pager template

Each case in Part 6 begins with a blank one-pager page in this format. The case owners fill it in from the detailed review, keeping it to one page. It is what the whole table reads the night before.

| Item | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Owners | Leads, counter-arguer, supporter |
| The case in three sentences | What happened, to whom, and what is at stake |
| The crux | The single moral tension, as "X versus Y" |
| The trap | The loaded comparison, double-edged fact, or two issues mashed into one |
| Most likely moderator question | Written as the moderator would ask it |
| Our answer in one sentence | The thesis, with its conditions |
| Claim 1 | One sentence |
| Claim 1 supports | S1, S2, S3, each with its backing in a few words |
| Claim 2 | One sentence |
| Claim 2 supports | S1, S2, S3, each with its backing in a few words |
| Counterclaim | Stated so its supporters would agree |
| Our response | Concede, distinguish, or rebut, in two sentences |
| What we concede | One line |
| Frameworks, in plain words | Name plus one sentence each |
| Three hardest judge questions | With a one-line answer each |
| If we comment on this case | The two gaps we'd raise in the most likely opposing claim |

## The detailed review template

Each case in Part 6 follows these sections.

| Section | What it holds | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The case at a glance | Every fact from the case set, outside facts marked as outside, what the case does not say | Lead 2 |
| 2. Crux, trap and stakeholders | The tension, the trap, and who gains, loses or goes unheard | Leads |
| 3. Moderator questions and our answer | Three to five versions of the question, one thesis that answers all of them | Whole team |
| 4. Claim 1 | Logic line, three supports with backing, ethical analysis | Lead 1 |
| 5. Claim 2 | Logic line, three supports with backing, ethical analysis | Lead 2 |
| 6. Counterclaims and our responses | The strongest opposing view at full strength; concede, distinguish or rebut | Counter-arguer |
| 7. From our practice notes | What the team and Dr. B said, and where it fits | Leads |
| 8. Judge question bank | Short answers to the questions judges are trained to ask | Whole team drills |
| 9. Commentary kit | What we'd raise if another team presents this case | Counter-arguer |
| 10. Check before December 5 | Facts to verify and open decisions | Supporter |
